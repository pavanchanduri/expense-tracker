Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you create — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or any financial account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling filtering by date and category. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) to operate the app, but these providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and do not have access to your data beyond what is necessary to run the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
